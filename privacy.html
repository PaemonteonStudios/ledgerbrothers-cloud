Privacy Policy

Cloud service. Effective November 5, 2026.

Draft for legal review. Items in [brackets] are to be confirmed before launch.

This policy covers the Ledger Brothers cloud service at cloud.ledgerbrothers.com (the "Service"), run by Paemonteon Studios, doing business as Ledger Brothers ("we", "us"). The downloadable ledger files, which keep data only on your own device, are covered by our separate policy on our product page.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we cannot read it. When you create an account we also record the version of these documents you agreed to and the time you agreed.
  • Your ledger data: the items, history and settings you enter into your ledgers, stored so they can sync between your devices.
  • Purchase details: when you buy through Gumroad, Gumroad tells us your email, the plan, the sale ID and whether the purchase is active, refunded or ended. We never receive your card number.
  • Basic usage events: when you sign in and which ledger you open, with a time stamp. We use these to run and improve the Service and to keep it secure. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting and database providers may log your IP address, browser type and request times.
  • Emails you send us: your address and what you write.

2. How we use it

To provide and secure the Service, sync your data, confirm your subscription, answer support requests, prevent abuse and fraud, keep records required by law, and send service emails (such as account confirmation and password reset). We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for advertising.

3. Emails we may send you

Service emails (confirmation, password reset, notices about your account or these policies) are part of the Service and are sent as needed. Reminder emails: if you create an account and have not subscribed, we may send you up to two short emails, at least three days apart, inviting you to subscribe. Each one names us, includes our postal address and has a one-click unsubscribe link. If you unsubscribe or reply "unsubscribe", we stop reminder emails to that address permanently. We keep the address on a do-not-email list for that purpose only. We never send reminder emails to anyone who has subscribed.

4. Who handles it for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage for the Service (hosted in the United States, East US / North Virginia).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the web pages of the Service.
  • Resend: sends the Service's emails. [Confirm provider before launch.]
  • Gumroad: takes payments and manages subscriptions.

These providers act for us and have their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect our rights, or in a sale or reorganisation of our business.

5. Cookies and browser storage

The Service does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It stores two small items in your browser, both strictly necessary, so no consent choice is needed:

NameWhereWhat it is for
supabase.auth.tokenLocal storageKeeps you signed in. Removed when you sign out.
lb_noticeLocal storageRemembers you dismissed the storage notice.

If we ever add anything that is not strictly necessary, we will ask for your permission first and update this table.

6. Who can see your ledger data

Database rules limit each ledger's data to the account that created it. Our administrators can see account-level information such as email, plan, last sign-in and how many items are stored, and their actions on accounts are logged. We do not read your ledger contents except to fix a problem you ask us to look at or where the law requires. Data is sent over encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, and if a breach affecting you occurs we will tell you as the law requires.

7. Keeping, exporting and deleting your data

Download: once signed in, use "Download my data" to get a copy of your account and ledger data. Delete: once signed in, use "Delete my account" to permanently delete your sign-in and your cloud ledger data. This takes effect immediately; copies in our provider's routine backups may remain for a limited time [confirm backup period with Supabase plan] and are then overwritten. When you delete, we keep one record that a deletion happened, containing only a one-way hash of your email and the date, so we can show the request was honoured. You can also email us from your account address and we will do it for you. Deleting your account does not cancel a Gumroad subscription; cancel it first (see the Terms).

Items you delete inside a ledger are marked deleted and permanently removed after 30 days. If your subscription ends we do not automatically delete your data; it stays stored but you cannot open the ledgers, and you can still download or delete it at any time. [Owner to decide whether to add automatic cleanup after a set period, and if so update this sentence.] Purchase and tax records may be kept up to seven years.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California and Texas, you may have the right to know what we hold about you, access or get a copy of it, correct it, delete it, and opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising (we do none of these). The download and delete buttons above cover access and deletion directly. For anything else email us from your account address; we will verify it is you and respond within the time the law requires, and if we decline a request you may appeal by replying to our answer. We will not treat you worse for using these rights.

9. Children

The Service is for businesses and adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International users

We are based in the United States and information is processed in the United States.

11. Do Not Track

We do not track you across websites, so we do not respond differently to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals.

12. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a new date. If a change is significant we will email account holders.
